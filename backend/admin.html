🔁 Monthly Subscription

A recurring alternative to the one-time packs below (added 2026-09). The main benefit besides ongoing credits: a subscriber's finished videos are kept for the full 30-day retention window instead of the short pay-once window (see the note under Credit Packs). Changing these doesn't affect anyone already subscribed at the old price — Stripe keeps existing subscribers on what they signed up for.

📊 Analytics

Paste either the bare ID (like G-XXXXXXXXXX) or the whole install snippet Google gives you — either works, we pull the ID out automatically. Turns on visitor tracking on the public landing page (lisanai.org). Leave blank to keep it off. Get a free ID at analytics.google.com.

💳 Credit Packs (one-time, pay-once)

Key is the pack's permanent public ID — it's what the landing page, buy modal and Stripe checkout use to identify this pack. Renaming the pack is safe and never changes its key. Leave Key blank on a new pack and it will be generated from the name when you save. Note: a pack buyer's finished videos are now only kept for PAYONCE_OUTPUT_HOURS (48h, set in main.py) — not the full 30 days — since they're not paying to keep the storage running. Only active Monthly Subscription users get the full 30-day retention.

Cloning a new speaker's voice fails once this hits the limit, even with character quota left. This is a live snapshot, not cumulative -- the app deletes each cloned voice once a job is done with it, so this number stays low even after heavy use.

Voice cloning credits

This is ElevenLabs' real monthly quota for creating/editing voices -- and it's the one that answers "how many clones do I have left."
