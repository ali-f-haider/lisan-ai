Everything You Need for Perfect Dubbing

Professional-grade AI tools that handle every step of the dubbing process automatically.

🎙️

Voice Cloning

Automatically extracts each speaker's unique voice from your video and creates a synthetic copy for the Arabic dub.

🌍

Smart Translation

AI translates English to natural-sounding Arabic, preserving context, tone, and meaning — not just word-for-word.

🎭

Emotion Detection

Analyzes voice tone to detect 31+ emotions and speaking styles. Combine tags like "confident, calm" for precise control.

⏱️

Auto Timing Sync

Automatically aligns Arabic speech to match the original timing. Fine-tune manually with our drag-and-drop timeline editor.

🔤

Arabic Tashkeel

Adds full diacritics (harakat) to Arabic text automatically, ensuring correct pronunciation by the voice engine.

🎬

Background Preservation

Separates vocals from background music/SFX, then mixes them back with the new Arabic audio at balanced volumes.

How It Works

From upload to final dubbed video in four simple steps.

1

Upload Your Video

Drop your MP4, AVI, MKV, or audio file. We support up to 60 seconds and 4K resolution. Our AI transcribes the English speech and identifies each speaker.

2

Review & Edit

Check the transcription, translate to Arabic with one click, add tashkeel, and let AI detect emotions. Edit anything manually if needed.

3

Assign Voices & Generate

Pick cloned voices or studio library voices for each speaker. Hit generate — the AI speaks every line in Arabic with the right emotion and timing.

4

Fine-Tune & Download

Use the timeline editor to perfectly align audio with lip movements. Merge with the original video and download your final dubbed result.
