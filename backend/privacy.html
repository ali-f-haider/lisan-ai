Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Lisan AI, we respect your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and what we don't.

1. What we store

We only store your account email, your credit balance, and your purchase history. We use Supabase to securely manage your account and Stripe to process payments.

2. What we DO NOT store

We do not keep copies of your uploaded videos, audio files, or generated dubbed media. All media files are processed temporarily on our servers and are automatically and permanently deleted within a few hours of processing or when the server recycles.

3. Payments

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see, store, or have access to your credit card numbers or banking details.

4. Your Content

You retain all rights to the content you upload. We do not use your uploaded videos to train AI models, nor do we share them with third parties.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about your data, contact us at ali_f_haider@hotmail.com.
