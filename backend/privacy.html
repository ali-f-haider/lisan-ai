Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Lisan AI ("we", "us") collects when you use our English-to-Arabic AI dubbing service, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, and your credit balance and purchase history.
  • Your content: the video/audio files you upload, the transcripts, translations, and generated Arabic audio/video produced from them.
  • Payment information: handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details — only the transaction result (amount, date, credits granted).
  • Basic technical data: standard web request data (such as IP address and browser type) generated automatically when you use the site, used only for security and troubleshooting.

How we use your information

  • To provide the dubbing service: transcription, translation, voice generation, and video merging.
  • To manage your account, credit balance, and purchases.
  • To communicate with you about your account or support requests.
  • To maintain and improve the reliability and security of the service.

We do not sell or share your content with third parties for their own marketing purposes, and we do not use your content to train AI models.

How long we keep your content

Your original upload and any in-progress working files (draft audio segments, temporary mixes) are automatically deleted from our servers after approximately 6 hours. How long your finished result — the generated audio and, if you merge or lip-sync it, the generated video — is kept depends on how you're paying: if you have an active monthly subscription, it's kept for up to 30 days so you can come back and download or delete it from your Account page, and we'll email you a reminder a few days before that automatic deletion happens; if you paid with a one-time credit purchase (or haven't purchased anything), it's kept for only 48 hours instead, with no separate reminder email, so please download what you want to keep promptly. Either way, deleting a file from your Account page removes it immediately, and it is then automatically deleted once its window passes. Your account email, credit balance, and purchase history are kept for as long as your account exists.

Third-party services we use

To provide the service, parts of your data are processed by these providers, each under their own privacy policy:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Supabase — account authentication and database hosting.
  • Google — "Sign in with Google" authentication.
  • Translation AI provider — used for translation, tashkeel, and emotion detection when selected.
  • Voice engine provider — used for AI voice generation and cloning.
  • Lip-sync provider — used to generate a lip-synced video when you choose that option (an alpha feature, available for short dubs only); your uploaded video is sent to this provider for that processing only.
  • Sentry — automated error monitoring so we can catch and fix bugs; receives limited technical error data, not your media content.
  • Cloudflare R2 — off-site backup storage for finished dubbing outputs.

Voice data & cloning

If you use voice cloning, the reference audio and the resulting cloned voice model are treated as biometric data. They're used only to generate your dubbing: old cloned voices are removed automatically rather than kept around indefinitely, and the isolated reference recording is deleted once your project resets or a new one starts.

Cookies & local storage

We use essential cookies/local storage only to keep you signed in and to remember your in-progress project. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Data security

Connections to the site are encrypted (HTTPS), and access to account and payment data is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data at any time by contacting us below. Deleting your account removes your stored account data; already-processed media is deleted automatically as described above regardless.

Children's privacy

Lisan AI is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
